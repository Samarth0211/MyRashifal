### Q: "What is Sade Sati and how to check if I'm going through it?"

Sade Sati is the 7.5-year period when Saturn transits through the sign before your Moon sign, your Moon sign itself, and the sign after it. It comes roughly every 30 years.

**Currently (2026):** If your Moon sign is Capricorn, Aquarius, or Pisces, you may be experiencing Sade Sati as Saturn transits through this area.

### Q: "How to read daily panchang?"

Panchang literally means "five limbs." It consists of:
1. **Tithi** — lunar day (Shukla/Krishna Paksha)
2. **Nakshatra** — Moon's constellation
3. **Yoga** — Sun-Moon angular relationship
4. **Karana** — half of a Tithi
5. **Vaar** — weekday

For daily use, check the Tithi (auspicious tithis like Dwitiya, Panchami, Dashami are good for new beginnings) and Rahu Kaal (avoid starting anything new during this period).
